BOOSIE NETWORK CREATOR PAYOUT & REVENUE SHARE POLICY
Effective Date: August 29, 2026

## 1. Purpose
This Policy explains the revenue-share splits and operational payout practices for eligible creators on the Boosie Network. It supplements, and is controlled by, the signed Artist & Creator Agreement. THE SPLITS DISPLAYED ON THE BOOSIE NETWORK PLANS PAGE AND CONFIRMED AT SIGN-UP ARE THE CONTROLLING RATES; this Policy reflects those exact rates.

## 2. Revenue Splits by Plan
The revenue share applied to a given monetized transaction is the rate attached to the creator's **active paid plan** at the time of that transaction.

## Artist Plan — $19.99 / month
- Music Revenue: 80% Creator / 20% Boosie Network
- Advertising Revenue (network-sourced): 60% Creator / 40% Boosie Network
- Advertising Revenue (creator-brought advertiser, accepted by Boosie Network): 70% Creator / 30% Boosie Network

## Artist Pro Plan — $49.99 / month
- Music Revenue: 85% Creator / 15% Boosie Network
- Merchandise Revenue: 87.5% Creator / 12.5% Boosie Network
- Advertising Revenue (network-sourced): 50% Creator / 50% Boosie Network
- Advertising Revenue (creator-brought advertiser, accepted by Boosie Network): 70% Creator / 30% Boosie Network

## Radio Rotation (Add-on, Separate Purchase)
Badazz Radio rotation is NOT a revenue split. It is an upfront, optional add-on purchase: $9.99/month on the Artist plan and $4.99/month on the Artist Pro plan. It purchases rotation placement; it does not change the splits above.

## Ticketing
For eligible ticketed events, the applicable Boosie Network service fee is the current fee posted at the point of sale for that event.

## 3. Net Revenue
Revenue shares are calculated on the applicable distributable amount after taxes, refunds, chargebacks, payment-processing charges, fraud losses, and transaction-specific third-party costs that are legally required or expressly disclosed. Boosie Network will not invent undisclosed deductions to alter an agreed split.

## 4. Ledger
Each monetized transaction is associated with gross amount, tax, fees, refunds/chargebacks, agreement version, creator share, network share, payout status, dates, and the related content, product, event, ad, or order. Creators can review their ledger in the "Run Me My Money" earnings dashboard.

## 5. Verification
Creators must complete required identity, tax, and payout (Stripe Connect) verification. Payments may be delayed until required information is complete.

## 6. Payout Timing
The platform displays the applicable payout schedule and expected settlement timing. Processor settlement, fraud review, returns, chargebacks, and reserves may delay availability. Boosie Network does not promise instant or fixed settlement unless the actual processor and operating model support it.

## 7. Holds and Reserves
Reasonable holds may apply to disputed rights, fraud indicators, refunds, chargebacks, negative balances, legal orders, processor reserves, or material agreement violations. The creator can see a meaningful status or explanation when legally and operationally appropriate.

## 8. Corrections
Boosie Network may correct ledger errors and offset verified overpayments, refunds, reversals, or negative balances. Material corrections are reflected in transaction history.

## 9. Taxes
Creators are responsible for their own income taxes except amounts Boosie Network must collect, withhold, report, or remit by law.

## 10. Rights Are Your Responsibility
Splits do not cover, and Boosie Network is not liable for, claims arising from content a creator does not own or have rights to. A creator who uploads unauthorized content is solely liable and indemnifies Boosie Network under the Content Rights Warranty & Ownership Attestation and the DMCA Policy.
