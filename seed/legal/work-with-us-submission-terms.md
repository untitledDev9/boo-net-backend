1. Scope
These Terms apply to submissions through Work With Us, including media, advertising, sponsorship, brand partnership, booking, event, film/production, licensing, music business, artist partnership, talent, creative professional, vendor, employment, Heartfelt, strategic, and general business inquiries.
2. No Obligation
Submitting an inquiry does not create a contract, booking, employment relationship, partnership, agency relationship, confidentiality obligation, or obligation to respond, negotiate, accept, purchase, invest, hire, license, or proceed.
3. Confidential Information
Do not submit trade secrets or information requiring confidentiality unless a written NDA or other confidentiality agreement has already been executed by an authorized Boosie Network representative. Boosie Network may receive similar ideas from multiple sources.
4. Accuracy and Authority
Submitters represent that information is accurate and that they have authority to submit materials, proposals, links, and contact information. They must have rights to any portfolio, music, video, deck, photograph, or other material supplied.
5. Internal Use
Boosie Network may internally review, route, store, summarize, assign, and evaluate submissions and may share them with authorized team members and service providers for that purpose, subject to the Privacy Policy.
6. Booking Inquiries
A booking inquiry is not a confirmed booking. No date is held and no appearance is confirmed until authorized parties execute the required agreement and required payments or deposits are completed.
7. Employment and Talent
Submitting employment, contractor, creative, or talent information does not guarantee consideration, audition, interview, engagement, or employment. Equal-employment obligations apply where legally required.
8. Unsolicited Creative Ideas
Unless specifically requested through an authorized submission program, Boosie Network does not agree to compensate unsolicited concepts merely because they are submitted. Submitters retain rights they otherwise hold, but should not submit confidential creative concepts without an applicable written agreement.
