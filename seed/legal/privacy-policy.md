BOOSIE NETWORK PRIVACY POLICY
Effective Date: August 29, 2026
BOOSIE NETWORK | LEGAL & POLICY
LEGAL REVIEW NOTE: Comprehensive operational draft. Have qualified counsel review and approve before public launch.
1. Scope
This Privacy Policy explains how Boosie Network collects, uses, discloses, retains, and protects personal information when people use our websites, applications, memberships, community, commerce, artist services, livestreams, radio, events, advertising, and internal-facing opportunity or application forms.
2. Information We Collect
We may collect account and profile information; contact information; age or date-of-birth information where needed; authentication data; purchase, subscription, order, refund, payout, and transaction information; creator, business, tax, and payout information; content and communications; community activity; livestream and radio interactions; event activity; advertising interactions; device, browser, IP, approximate location, logs, cookies, identifiers, and analytics; customer-support records; application, audition, booking, vendor, employment, partnership, and Work With Us submissions; and information you choose to provide.
3. How We Use Information
We use information to provide and personalize Services; authenticate users; process transactions; fulfill orders; administer memberships; operate artist and creator programs; calculate and administer revenue shares and payouts; deliver livestreams and media; moderate and secure the platform; prevent fraud; provide support; communicate service and marketing messages; operate advertising and attribution; analyze performance; improve products; comply with law; enforce agreements; protect rights and safety; and support business operations.
4. AI Features
When authorized users interact with AI-assisted features such as Little Boo or other Boosie Network AI tools, prompts, relevant authorized platform context, and generated outputs may be processed to provide the feature, maintain security, and improve reliability. Role-based permissions must limit what internal AI features can retrieve or reveal. We do not represent AI output as guaranteed accurate and users should review important outputs.
5. How We Disclose Information
We may disclose information to service providers and processors that support hosting, payments, fraud prevention, email, communications, analytics, customer support, content delivery, streaming, shipping, advertising, security, and professional services; to artists or business partners when necessary to fulfill a user-directed transaction or interaction; in a corporate transaction; with consent; or when required to comply with law, enforce rights, prevent harm, or protect the platform.
6. Public Information
Profile information, posts, comments, reactions, follower relationships, artist pages, public livestream activity, and other information intentionally made public may be visible to others and may be shared outside Boosie Network. Users should not post information they wish to keep private.
7. Advertising and Analytics
We may use first-party and third-party technologies to measure traffic, engagement, conversions, campaign performance, and advertising effectiveness. Depending on configuration and applicable law, users may have choices regarding cookies, targeted advertising, or sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising. The final production configuration should provide legally required consent and opt-out mechanisms.
8. Payments and Financial Data
Payment card information may be collected and processed by authorized payment processors rather than stored directly by Boosie Network. Creator payout and tax information may be processed by authorized financial providers. We retain transaction and ledger records as reasonably necessary for accounting, disputes, fraud prevention, contractual obligations, and law.
9. Data Retention
We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including providing Services, maintaining transaction and rights records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, preventing abuse, and meeting legal obligations. Retention periods vary by data type and legal requirement.
10. Security
We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information, including access controls, authentication, logging, separation of sensitive information, and secure service providers. No system can guarantee absolute security.
11. Your Choices and Rights
Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy of, or restrict certain uses of personal information; opt out of certain targeted advertising, sale, or sharing; withdraw consent; or appeal certain privacy decisions. We may verify identity before fulfilling a request. Authorized agents may submit requests where permitted by law.
12. U.S. State Privacy Rights
Residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws may have additional rights. Boosie Network will honor applicable rights and will not discriminate against users for exercising legally protected privacy rights. The exact state-specific disclosures and opt-out signals should be aligned to the production data flows before launch.
13. Children
Boosie Network is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without legally valid authorization. Features involving minors, including auditions or talent submissions, must use appropriate parent or guardian consent and privacy controls.
14. International Users
If information is transferred across borders, Boosie Network and its providers will use appropriate mechanisms where required. Users outside the United States may have additional rights under local law.
15. Changes and Re-Consent
We may update this Policy. Material changes will be communicated as required. Where a change requires consent, or where migrated users must accept materially updated terms, Boosie Network may require affirmative re-consent at next login before continued use of affected features.
16. Contact
Privacy requests and questions may be sent to legal@boosienetwork.com.
