# 1. Security

Do not probe, scan, attack, bypass, disable, overload, reverse engineer where prohibited, introduce malicious code, steal credentials, evade rate limits, or access accounts or systems without authorization.

# 2. Automation

Do not scrape, crawl, bulk-download, automate account creation, manipulate engagement, or access the Services through bots or scripts except through expressly authorized interfaces or written permission.

# 3. Fraud and Payments

Do not use stolen payment methods, launder funds, create sham transactions, abuse refunds, coordinate chargeback fraud, falsify payout identity, or manipulate platform revenue.

# 4. Illegal and Harmful Activity

Do not use the Services to facilitate unlawful goods or services, exploitation, threats, extortion, fraud, trafficking, or other prohibited conduct.

# 5. Platform Integrity

Do not manipulate rankings, charts, votes, Battle of the Catalog results, ad metrics, plays, views, follows, comments, ratings, or other platform measurements.

# 6. Enforcement

Violations may result in rate limits, feature restrictions, removal, suspension, termination, withheld disputed amounts where legally appropriate, or legal action.
