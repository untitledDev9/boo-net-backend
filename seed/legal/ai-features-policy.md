# 1. Scope

This Policy governs AI-assisted features including Little Boo, owner intelligence, creator assistance, drafting, summaries, analytics explanations, recommendations, campaign ideas, and other AI-enabled workflows.

# 2. AI Can Be Wrong

AI-generated information may be incomplete, inaccurate, outdated, or inappropriate. Users must review important outputs before relying on them, especially financial, legal, safety, employment, contractual, rights, or business decisions.

# 3. Permissions

Internal AI must honor the same or stricter role-based access controls as the underlying application. An employee may not obtain information through AI that the employee is not authorized to access directly.

# 4. Sensitive Actions

AI may assist with drafts, summaries, analysis, and proposed actions. Material actions such as sending sensitive communications, changing financial records, approving payouts, modifying contracts, deleting data, publishing high-impact content, or changing permissions require appropriate user intent, authorization, and confirmation.

# 5. Inputs

Users should not enter information they are not authorized to disclose. Internal AI may use authorized platform context to answer role-relevant questions. Prompts and outputs may be logged for security, quality, and audit purposes as disclosed in the Privacy Policy.

# 6. No Professional Advice

AI output is not legal, medical, tax, accounting, or other regulated professional advice. Qualified professionals should review decisions requiring such expertise.

# 7. Human Accountability

The authorized user remains responsible for reviewing and approving actions taken using AI assistance.
