BOOSIE NETWORK COMMUNITY GUIDELINES
Effective Date: August 29, 2026
BOOSIE NETWORK | LEGAL & POLICY
LEGAL REVIEW NOTE: Comprehensive operational draft. Have qualified counsel review and approve before public launch.
1. Our Standard
Boosie Network is built for culture, conversation, artists, fans, entertainment, and community. Strong opinions, jokes, debate, criticism, and authentic expression are welcome. Threats, exploitation, targeted harassment, illegal activity, and conduct that makes the platform unsafe are not.
2. Safety
Do not threaten credible violence, encourage self-harm, coordinate attacks, dox people, share private identifying information to facilitate harm, or use the platform to facilitate criminal activity.
3. Harassment
Do not engage in repeated targeted abuse, sexual harassment, stalking, extortion, blackmail, or coordinated harassment. Criticism of public figures and public conduct may be permitted when it does not cross into prohibited threats or targeted abuse.
4. Hate and Dehumanization
Do not attack or dehumanize people based on protected characteristics, promote violent hatred, or use the platform to organize discriminatory harm.
5. Sexual Exploitation and Minors
Sexual exploitation, non-consensual intimate imagery, grooming, sexual content involving minors, and any material that exploits children are prohibited and may be reported to appropriate authorities.
6. Authenticity and Fraud
Do not impersonate others deceptively, run scams, manipulate engagement, sell fake goods, solicit fraudulent payments, or misrepresent affiliation with Boosie, Boosie Network, artists, staff, or partners.
7. Intellectual Property
Post only content you have the right to share. Rights holders may use the Copyright Policy process. Repeat infringement can lead to account termination.
8. Spam and Manipulation
Do not flood feeds, comments, chats, messages, polls, votes, or follows with automated or coordinated manipulation. Artificial streaming, fake engagement, and metric manipulation are prohibited.
9. Livestream Conduct
Hosts are responsible for guests and content appearing in their streams. Moderators may mute, remove, slow, restrict, or terminate chats or streams to enforce these Guidelines.
10. Enforcement
Depending on severity, context, history, and risk, enforcement may include warning, content removal, reduced distribution, feature restriction, demonetization, temporary suspension, permanent ban, or referral to law enforcement where appropriate. We may preserve relevant records.
11. Appeals
Where an appeal process is available, users may request review. Repeated abusive appeals or attempts to evade enforcement may be restricted.
