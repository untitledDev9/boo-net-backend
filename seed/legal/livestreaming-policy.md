BOOSIE NETWORK LIVESTREAMING POLICY
Effective Date: August 29, 2026
BOOSIE NETWORK | LEGAL & POLICY
LEGAL REVIEW NOTE: Comprehensive operational draft. Have qualified counsel review and approve before public launch.
1. Eligibility
Livestreaming is available only to accounts and plans approved for LIVE access. Access may depend on identity verification, account standing, technical readiness, rights compliance, and platform capacity.
2. Rights
Streamers must have rights to all music, video, performances, artwork, guests, broadcasts, and other protected material included in a stream. A creator plan does not grant licenses to third-party music or broadcasts.
3. Safety and Conduct
Community Guidelines apply in full. Streamers must not broadcast illegal conduct, exploitation, prohibited sexual content, credible threats, private information intended to facilitate harm, or other prohibited material.
4. Guests and Moderation
Hosts are responsible for reasonable management of guests and chat. Boosie Network may provide moderators and tools including mute, block, slow mode, subscriber-only mode, removal, and stream termination.
5. Recording and Replays
Streams may be recorded or retained for replays, moderation, safety, rights enforcement, analytics, and legal compliance as disclosed through the product. Hosts should inform guests where legally required.
6. Monetization
PPV, commerce, ads, gifts, subscriptions, or other monetization may be available only to eligible accounts and may be subject to additional economics, fees, refunds, and payment rules.
7. Boosie Drop-In
Where the Boosie Drop-In feature is enabled, eligible streams may permit the owner or authorized account to join according to displayed platform controls. The feature should provide a clear on-stream indicator and maintain appropriate permission and audit records.
8. Enforcement
Boosie Network may interrupt, limit, demonetize, remove, or terminate a stream for policy, safety, legal, rights, fraud, or technical reasons.
