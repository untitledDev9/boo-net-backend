# 1. What We Use

Boosie Network may use cookies, pixels, local storage, SDKs, server logs, and similar technologies to authenticate users, remember preferences, secure accounts, measure performance, understand usage, personalize experiences, attribute campaigns, and support advertising.

# 2. Categories

Technologies may be categorized as strictly necessary, functional, analytics/performance, and advertising/targeting depending on production configuration.

# 3. Choices

Where required by law, non-essential technologies will be subject to consent or opt-out controls. Users may also use browser or device controls, though disabling certain technologies may affect functionality.

# 4. Global Privacy Control

Where legally required and technically applicable, Boosie Network should recognize supported opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control for relevant processing.

# 5. Third Parties

Third-party providers may set or receive identifiers when providing payment, analytics, advertising, media, security, or other services. The production cookie inventory should identify actual providers before launch.
