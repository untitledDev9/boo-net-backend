# 1. Mission

Boosie Network Culture covers music, entertainment, artists, live moments, community, business, and culture with a direct voice while distinguishing reporting, commentary, sponsored content, and official Boosie Network statements.

# 2. Accuracy

Writers and editors should verify material factual claims using reliable sources, distinguish allegations from established facts, seek primary sources where practical, and avoid presenting rumor as fact.

# 3. Opinion and Perspective

Commentary may be forceful and may present Boosie's or another contributor's viewpoint, but disputed factual assertions should be attributed to the person making them. Headlines should not materially misstate the underlying article.

# 4. Conflicts and Sponsorship

Paid or sponsored content must be disclosed. Material conflicts of interest should be disclosed when relevant to reader understanding.

# 5. Corrections

Material factual errors should be corrected promptly. Significant corrections should include an appropriate correction or update note rather than silently rewriting the historical record where transparency is warranted.

# 6. AI Assistance

AI may assist with research organization, drafting, transcription, headlines, or editing, but publication requires human review. AI-generated claims should not be published as verified facts without appropriate verification.

# 7. Fictional or Pen-Name Bylines

Boosie Network should not invent false professional credentials, employment histories, interviews, or firsthand experiences for a fictional persona. If editorial pen names are used, internal records should identify the responsible human/editorial owner and the publication should not use the pen name to fabricate authority.
