BOOSIE NETWORK REFUND, RETURN & CANCELLATION POLICY
Effective Date: August 29, 2026
BOOSIE NETWORK | LEGAL & POLICY
LEGAL REVIEW NOTE: Comprehensive operational draft. Have qualified counsel review and approve before public launch.
1. Memberships and Creator Plans
Recurring memberships and creator plans may be canceled through the available account controls. Cancellation prevents the next renewal. Unless required by law or expressly stated at checkout, charges for a billing period already started are non-refundable. If a service is materially unavailable due to Boosie Network and no reasonable substitute is provided, Boosie Network may issue a credit or refund as appropriate.
2. Digital Content
Purchases of digital music, video access, rentals, streams, PPV, and other digital content are generally final once access, download, or streaming has begun, except where content is defective, unavailable, incorrectly charged, or applicable law requires a refund.
3. Physical Merchandise
Eligible unworn, unused, and unwashed merchandise may be returned within 30 days after delivery unless marked final sale, customized, intimate/hygiene-sensitive, or otherwise excluded at checkout. Items should be returned in original condition. Original shipping charges are generally non-refundable unless the item was defective, damaged, or incorrect. Return shipping responsibility should be disclosed during the return process.
4. Damaged or Incorrect Orders
Customers should report damaged, defective, or incorrect physical orders promptly with order details and reasonable evidence. Boosie Network may replace, refund, or otherwise resolve verified issues.
5. Events and Tickets
Ticket refunds are governed by the applicable event terms. Unless an event is canceled, postponed, materially changed, or applicable law requires otherwise, ticket sales may be final. Event-specific policies control where they differ. Third-party venue, promoter, travel, parking, lodging, or add-on terms may also apply.
6. Chargebacks
Customers should contact support before initiating a chargeback when possible. Fraudulent or abusive chargebacks may result in account restrictions. Nothing here limits lawful dispute rights.
7. Refund Method
Approved refunds are generally returned to the original payment method. Processing time depends on the payment provider and financial institution.
8. Contact
Refund and order questions may be directed through Boosie Network support or legal@boosienetwork.com.
