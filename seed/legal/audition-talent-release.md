# 1. Submission

Applicant voluntarily submits performance, identity, contact, biography, images, video, audio, links, and other audition materials for consideration by Boosie Network or an authorized production.

# 2. Rights in Submission

Applicant retains ownership of materials Applicant owns but grants Boosie Network a non-exclusive license to copy, review, internally share, store, format, and display the submission to authorized personnel for evaluation, casting, production planning, and related administration.

# 3. No Guarantee

Submission does not guarantee an audition, callback, role, appearance, employment, payment, publication, or other opportunity.

# 4. Applicant Representations

Applicant represents that submitted materials are truthful and that Applicant has rights and permissions necessary to submit them. Applicant will not submit material that unlawfully infringes another person's rights.

# 5. Minors

Applicants under the age of majority must have a parent or legal guardian complete required consent and release documentation. Minor information must be handled with enhanced privacy controls and not publicly displayed without appropriate authorization.

# 6. Recording Release

If Applicant participates in a recorded audition, callback, or production activity, additional appearance, performance, publicity, compensation, union, or production releases may be required. This submission release alone does not replace deal-specific production paperwork.

# 7. Privacy

Submission information is handled according to the Privacy Policy and is private by default, accessible only to authorized personnel and service providers supporting the process.
