1. Orders
Orders are subject to payment authorization, inventory, fraud screening, shipping restrictions, and acceptance. An order confirmation does not guarantee fulfillment if inventory or pricing errors are discovered.
2. Product Information
Boosie Network and participating sellers should provide reasonable product descriptions, pricing, variants, and availability. Images may differ slightly from physical products due to screens, manufacturing, or production variation.
3. Shipping
Shipping methods, estimated delivery windows, costs, and geographic availability are displayed when available. Delivery dates are estimates unless expressly guaranteed. Carrier delays and events outside reasonable control may affect delivery.
4. Creator Merchandise
Products sold by participating creators may be fulfilled by Boosie Network, the creator, or an approved fulfillment provider as disclosed. The party responsible for fulfillment should be identifiable in internal order records and customer support workflows.
5. Returns
Returns are governed by the Refund, Return & Cancellation Policy and any product-specific final-sale terms displayed before purchase.
6. Taxes and Duties
Applicable sales tax may be collected. International customers may be responsible for customs, duties, or import charges unless checkout states otherwise.
