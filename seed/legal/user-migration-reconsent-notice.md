# 1. Why You Are Seeing This

Boosie Network may migrate existing accounts into a materially expanded platform with updated services, memberships, community features, commerce, media, privacy practices, and account capabilities.

# 2. Review Required

Where appropriate, migrated users will be asked at first login to review and affirmatively accept the current Terms of Service and Privacy Policy before continuing to use affected authenticated features. Creator accounts must also accept the applicable current Artist & Creator Agreement before creator monetization continues.

# 3. Existing Purchases and Rights

Migration does not intentionally erase valid completed purchases, subscriptions, balances, or contractual rights. Historical transactions should remain associated with the terms and agreement version applicable when they occurred, subject to lawful corrections, refunds, chargebacks, or separately agreed changes.

# 4. Declining

A user who does not accept required updated terms may be unable to use affected account features. The user should be given legally appropriate options to cancel recurring services, request account/data assistance, or exercise applicable privacy rights.

# 5. Recordkeeping

The system should record the agreement version, timestamp, user/account identifier, and other legally appropriate evidence of acceptance.
