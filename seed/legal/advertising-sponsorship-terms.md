1. Scope
These Terms apply to advertisers, agencies, sponsors, and brands purchasing or arranging advertising, sponsored content, audio, video, live, event, Culture, artist, homepage, takeover, email, or other promotional placements through Boosie Network.
2. Approval
All campaigns are subject to approval. Boosie Network may reject, pause, or remove advertising that violates law, platform standards, rights, safety requirements, or brand suitability standards.
3. Advertiser Responsibilities
Advertiser represents that its ads, offers, claims, landing pages, products, targeting instructions, intellectual property, endorsements, and required disclosures comply with applicable law and industry rules. Advertiser must substantiate objective claims and obtain required permissions.
4. Prohibited or Restricted Categories
Boosie Network may prohibit or restrict categories including unlawful products, deceptive financial schemes, weapons, illegal drugs, exploitative sexual content, counterfeit goods, hate-related products, malware, scams, and other high-risk categories. Additional age, location, licensing, or disclosure rules may apply to regulated categories.
5. Campaign Delivery
Impressions, clicks, plays, views, conversions, and other metrics may be affected by fraud filtering, technical conditions, user choices, measurement limits, and third-party systems. Unless a signed insertion order states a guaranteed delivery, estimates are not guarantees.
6. Creator Revenue Sharing
Where a campaign is eligible for artist revenue sharing, the applicable creator agreement and campaign attribution records determine the split. Current working economics are 50/50 for eligible platform-sourced artist advertising and 70% Creator / 30% Network when an accepted advertiser is directly originated by the Creator.
7. Payment
Advertiser must pay fees according to checkout, invoice, or insertion-order terms. Taxes and approved third-party production costs may be additional. Late, reversed, fraudulent, or disputed payments may pause campaigns.
8. Sponsored Content
Sponsored or paid content must be identified with disclosures appropriate to the format and applicable law. Editorial independence should not be represented falsely.
9. Make-Goods and Cancellations
Campaign cancellation, underdelivery, and make-good terms should follow the applicable order. Where no signed order controls, Boosie Network may provide a reasonable credit, replacement inventory, or refund for material undelivery caused by Boosie Network.
10. Data
Advertisers may receive aggregated or campaign-level reporting but may not receive personal information except as legally permitted and specifically authorized. Advertisers may not use Boosie Network data to unlawfully discriminate, re-identify users, or build unauthorized profiles.
