# 1. Commitment

Boosie Network is committed to making its digital experiences usable by as many people as reasonably possible, including people with disabilities.

# 2. Practices

We aim to support semantic structure, keyboard navigation, readable contrast, alternative text, captions where appropriate, focus visibility, form labels, responsive design, and reduced-motion preferences.

# 3. Continuous Improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process. New media, community content, third-party integrations, and rapidly changing features may create issues that require correction.

# 4. Feedback

Users who encounter an accessibility barrier may contact legal@boosienetwork.com with the page, feature, device, browser, and a description of the issue so the team can investigate.
